Grub Grab

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Grub Grab is a small app for keeping recipes and building a shared shopping list for a household. It is run by an individual for personal and family use, not by a company. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, how it is stored and shared, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

From your Google account

Grub Grab uses Sign in with Google. When you sign in, Google shares the following with the app, and nothing else:

  • your name,
  • your email address (and whether Google has verified it),
  • a unique account identifier that tells the app it's the same person next time.

Grub Grab only asks for the basic openid, email and profile permissions. It cannot see your Gmail, contacts, calendar, files, password or anything else in your Google account.

What you add to the app

  • Recipes: titles, ingredients, quantities, notes, tags and links.
  • Your household's shopping list.
  • Your household's invite code, and which household you belong to.
  • Links to recipe pages you ask the app to read with Fill from link, and a monthly count of how many your household has used.

Collected automatically

  • Request logs: the time, your account identifier, IP address, browser type and which part of the app was used, with the result. They never contain your recipes, list or sign-in tokens. They are used to keep the service secure and working.

Grub Grab does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Your session is kept in your browser's storage on your own device so that you stay signed in.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and recognise you on later visits.
  • To approve new accounts: when someone signs in for the first time, their name and email are shown to the app's administrator, who decides whether to let them in. The administrator receives an email notification of new requests.
  • To show your account in Settings (your email).
  • To run the app: storing your recipes and list and sharing them with the members of your household.
  • To fill in a recipe from a link, when you choose to: the recipe page is read by an AI model (see "Who we share it with") and the result is shown to you to check before anything is saved.
  • To keep it secure and to investigate faults or abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles of you.

Use of Google user data

Grub Grab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information from your Google account is used only to sign you in, identify you within the app and approve your access, as described above. It is never sent to an AI model or AI provider (including for Fill from link, which sends only the recipe page's link), it is not transferred to anyone except as described in "Who we share it with", it is not used for advertising, and no person reads it except the administrator, who sees new sign-up requests, or where needed for security or to comply with the law.

Who we share it with

  • Members of your household see the household's recipes, shopping list and invite code. They do not see your email address.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) hosts the app and stores its data in its London (eu-west-2) region, acting as a service provider. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito together with Google.
  • Google handles the sign-in itself, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic, which provides the Claude AI model, receives the link when you use Fill from link, fetches that public page and returns the recipe's ingredients. Nothing about you or your Google account is sent with it. Anthropic processes these requests as a service provider under its commercial terms and privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not share it with anyone else unless required by law.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, recipes and list: until you delete your account.
  • Unanswered sign-up requests: deleted automatically after 30 days. Declined requests: kept for 1 year so the same account isn't asked about again.
  • Request logs: 90 days.
  • Database backups, kept for recovery from accidental loss, roll over within 35 days, so deleted data disappears from them after at most 35 days.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete my account. This immediately removes your sign-in, your profile and your sign-up request. If you are the last member of your household, the household's recipes, shopping list and invite code are deleted too. If other people are still in your household, the shared recipes and list stay with them.

You can also ask for deletion, or for a copy of your data, by emailing {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You can stop Grub Grab's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account's third-party connections.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request to the app's data must carry a valid sign-in, and each household can only read its own data. New accounts need the administrator's approval. Access to the hosting account is protected by multi-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

Children

Grub Grab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK or EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your information, or to object to how it is used. Email us to make a request; we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes to this policy
